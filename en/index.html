My story

Still a work in progress

Hi, I'm Meli. Mum of a little boy and a little girl, I now work from home, after years in the hotel industry with irregular hours. El Salvador: yes or no?

In 2019, I decided to change the way I lived. I threw myself into exercise, and not just doing it: I wanted to learn. How to breathe, how to position my body, which postures to use so every exercise does good instead of harm. what made you want to change

Then came my two pregnancies. After my postpartum, I had to rebuild myself, gently. That's when supplements became part of my routine, and they've stayed there.

I don't have a before and after photo to show you, and that's on purpose. I haven't "arrived": I keep going, one day at a time, and I show my tummy as it is today.

What I want is to help mums who got a little lost along the way find themselves again. No miracle promises, just moving forward together.
